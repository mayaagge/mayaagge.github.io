Bloons Tower Defense

A browser version of the Processing game I built with a team. Same path, rounds, towers, and costs as the original; the code was translated from Processing (Java) to JavaScript so it runs here without installing anything.

How to play
  • Press any key or click to start. You begin with 2000 money and 10 lives.
  • Press M (or the button) to open the monkey menu, click a monkey, then click the map to place it. Towers can't go on the path or on top of each other.
  • Dart 200, Sniper 300, Engineer 600 (drops sentries), Wizard 800 (chain lightning), Dartling 800 (aim it with your mouse).
  • Popped bloons give money. A bloon that reaches the end costs a life. Survive 10 rounds to win.
