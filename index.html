3-phase AC induction motor simulation

An interactive model of a stator and squirrel-cage rotor. Sliders change voltage, frequency, rotor resistance, load torque, inertia, damping, and rotor size, and live graphs show currents, rotor speed, EMF, and torque as the motor spins up.

Why it was hard
The physics chains together: three phase currents make a rotating field, the field induces current in the rotor (Faraday's law), and that current produces torque. Getting slip and net torque to settle correctly took careful time-stepping.
What I did
Built the simulation for a physics project, with a 3D view, camera controls, pause and reset, and four live graphs.
What I'd improve
Check the simulated torque and slip against a real motor's datasheet, and move the simulation into the browser so anyone can run it without installing Python.
